# Hypothesis research pilot protocol

Status: prepared protocol; not recruited, registered, or executed. Related task:
[T17 / #25](https://github.com/bknstn/idea-maze-engine/issues/25). Synthetic
regression results do not substitute for this pilot.

## Registration before recruitment

The coordinator and owner must record the release commit, dates, participant
count (5–10), comparison client/model, equal per-workflow time and provider
budgets, total spending ceiling, allowed data, retention deadline, consent wording,
and private storage location. Keep identities, consent records, ideas, research
reports, and raw feedback outside this repository. A protocol with unresolved
budget or data fields is not a registered live study.

Recruit consenting adults who already investigate product or workflow hypotheses.
Explain that selected idea details, queries, and original-source text can reach
configured providers. Participants may use fictional or non-confidential ideas,
withdraw before aggregation, and decline individual questions. Do not accept
employer/client secrets, credentials, or third-party personal data. Recruitment,
participant messaging, and provider spending need an explicit execution scope.

## Paired comparison

Use one participant-selected, permitted hypothesis per participant and two
workflows: ordinary chat research and this release's hypothesis research service.
Randomly assign which workflow runs first; record the assignment before either
session. Supply the same initial description and hypothesis to both, use separate
sessions, and retain the same time and spending limits. Record search/tool access
and model differences; do not conceal capability differences behind an equal
budget claim. Do not feed the first workflow's findings into the second session.

Before starting, privately record the participant's current hypothesis, confidence
in ordinary language, intended next action, and prior sources. At each stop record
elapsed time, observed requests/tokens, and reported cost. Unknown cost stays
unknown; choose an external provider/account spending control if a hard money
ceiling is required. Stop on consent withdrawal, budget/time exhaustion, unsafe
data disclosure, or provider failure. Preserve the stop reason and partial result.

After both sessions, ask the participant privately:

1. Which original sources were useful, and why?
2. Did either result change a concrete hypothesis, decision, or next experiment?
   Record the before/after action rather than counting a general positive comment.
3. Which workflow would they reuse for a similar task, or neither, and why?
4. What was missing or misleading, including counterevidence and collection gaps?

## Independent report review

A reviewer who did not author the reports reviews both outputs with workflow
labels hidden where practical. Check every cited quotation against its captured
original version, exact text/range and source reference. Separately assess whether
the relationship and scope support the associated conclusion. Provenance alone
does not establish interpretation, buyer identity, frequency, or demand.

Count useful original sources after grouping duplicates/dependent material; count
fabricated quotations, distinct unsupported factual conclusions, relevant
counterevidence included/omitted, and visible coverage limitations. Keep the
coding decisions and disagreements privately, with source references. If no
conclusions can be reviewed, report unavailable rather than zero unsupported
conclusions. Ask a second reviewer to adjudicate disagreements without changing
registered targets. Participants' private feedback remains separate from the
reviewer's evidence assessment.

## Registered provisional decision rule

Before recruitment, confirm these provisional targets from #25 or record a dated
amendment: zero fabricated quotations in reviewed reports; at least 60% of
participants identify a concrete decision or experiment changed by the service
result; at least 50% prefer the service to their baseline. Report counts and
denominators alongside percentages, including missing responses and withdrawals.
With 5–10 participants these observations are directional, not population estimates.

Recommend **continue** only if all targets hold and no unresolved material
provenance/privacy failure remains. Recommend **change and repeat** for missed
targets with a specific, testable remedy. Recommend **stop** if the workflow causes
unacceptable disclosure or repeated fabricated provenance, or has no actionable
benefit under the comparison budget. Explain deviations and capability differences
instead of treating the rule as statistical proof.

## Public completion artifact

Publish only anonymized aggregate counts, unavailable fields, budget comparability,
limitations, protocol deviations, and the continue/change/stop recommendation.
Suppress small-group details that could identify a participant. Do not publish
raw reports, quotes from interviews, participant identifiers, ideas, or source
collections. Review the aggregate against `OPEN_SOURCE_REPO_SAFETY.md` before
committing. Delete private working copies on the agreed retention deadline while
preserving only consented, necessary aggregate evidence.

Issue #25 remains open until actual consented sessions and aggregate review are
complete. No live-pilot outcome is claimed by preparing this protocol.
